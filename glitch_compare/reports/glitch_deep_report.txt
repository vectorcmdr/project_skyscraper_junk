ARG CLUES & ANOMALIES - GLITCH DEEP ANALYSIS
============================================================

1. SKYSCRAPER PERIODICITY:
   Dominant row period = 480px, col period = 77px.
   480 = 2400/5. This suggests the 2400x2400 skyscraper glitch may be
   tiled or generated on a 5x5 grid basis. 77px is close to 2400/31.
   The glitches are NOT random - they follow a mathematical pattern.

2. COMMON GLITCH ALGORITHM:
   Both skyscraper and IMG_00004 show ~5% high-variance pixels.
   Histogram chi2 values are LOW (0.39-0.69), indicating the "glitch"
   regions in both images have very similar statistical distributions.
   This is strong evidence that the SAME glitch algorithm / filter
   was applied to both images, despite their different content.
   This makes sense given the similar banding visual clues observed.

3. DCT ANOMALIES:
   - Skyscraper: 293 anomalous blocks (1.3% of 22500)
   - IMG_00004: 76 anomalous blocks (2.2% of 3450)
   The anomalous blocks represent regions where DCT high-frequency
   energy is >3 standard deviations above mean. These are the actual
   "glitched" macroblocks.

4. NON-STANDARD MARKERS in SKYSCRAPER:
   Found 11 instances of 0xFFC2 (Start-of-Frame markers) and 0xFFC4
   (Huffman table markers) at positions that are NOT at segment
   boundaries. This suggests the JPEG structure itself may have been
   manipulated - possibly multiple SOF/DHT segments concatenated, or
   DCT coefficients directly modified.

5. NO HIDDEN TEXT IN LSB:
   Extracted LSB from glitched regions of IMG_00004 yields only
   garbage (no ASCII text). The corruption does not appear to be
   steganographic LSB encoding, or requires a key (likely if real).

6. BITPLANE ANALYSIS:
   Saved bitplanes 0 (LSB), 1, and 7 (MSB) for both images.
   Check bitplane_0 and bitplane_1 for hidden patterns - sometimes
   ARG creators embed data in lower bitplanes while destroying
